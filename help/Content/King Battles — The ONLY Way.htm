King Battles — The ONLY Way to Win

Since there's no checkmate, capturing the enemy King is the only way to actually win the game. Attack a King, and instead of one card battle, you get a special multi-round showdown.

Know these before you attack a King:

  • Up to 3 rounds, best score wins. If you win the first two rounds in a row, that's already the majority — round 3 gets skipped because it can't change anything.
  • No sudden death, no do-overs. A 1-1-1 tie (only possible if all three rounds happen) means nothing moves.
  • Snipers and Armageddon don't do their special tricks during a King battle. A Sniper drawn here just wins that one round, like a super-high card — no bonus piece, no Armageddon.
  • If your King attacks something and loses, your King is captured immediately — YOU LOSE THE ENTIRE GAME. Attack with your King carefully.
